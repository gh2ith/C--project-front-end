# Bridgeway internship process film

Audience: Yarmouk IT students and internship staff. Goal: explain the complete required cycle in 34 seconds. Emotion: clear next steps and confidence about each person's responsibility.

Brand inventory: original blue bridge/cap mark, Inter/Montserrat typography, white task surfaces, blue action buttons, navy text. The central action is passing one piece of work from student to company to academic staff and completion. The successful moment is the approved certificate.

Considered directions: a literal bridge journey, a typography-only explainer, and one carried work record. Chosen: the carried record, because the viewer watches applications, work and review change state instead of reading a list of features. The homepage already owns the physical bridge journey.

World: a lightly textured drafting ground, daylight from the upper right, layered white paper with soft depth and Bridgeway blue for actions. Crisp motion uses measured soft-land arrivals. No music/voice service is configured, so this is a silent captioned explainer. The sound choice is intentional and no audio is promised.

| Time | Beat | What the viewer learns | Transition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5 | Opportunity → Apply | Full opportunity access and application after login | Cursor presses the existing blue action |
| 5–11 | Pending → Accepted → Confirm | Company decides; student confirms one offer | Application paper becomes accepted offer |
| 11–16 | Assigned task → submission | Work has deadlines and task-specific criteria | Same paper becomes a practical task |
| 16–22 | Feedback → revision → evaluated | Company requests improvements; submission history stays | Revision sheet replaces the feedback |
| 22–27 | Hours verification | Company records; academic supervisor verifies separately | Work record becomes verified-hours record |
| 27–32 | Two final evaluations | Company → academic → coordinator | One carried paper becomes the review chain |
| 32–34 | Approved certificate | Completion message and certificate after approval | Final paper receives the original Bridgeway mark |

Hero moment: the task feedback becomes the revised submission, then the paper resolves into the certificate. The last shot is 2 seconds, less than a quarter of the runtime. Text is magnified and centered on one dominant paper surface; no page chrome or competing panels.

This is an authored illustration of the frontend workflow, not recorded evidence of actual student work, a real university approval, or externally sent evaluations.
