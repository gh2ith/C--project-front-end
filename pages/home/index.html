02 Seamless Applications

Apply. Receive an offer. Confirm your place.

Submit your university student profile to the host company. The company reviews your application and decides whether to accept or reject it.

  • A dedicated application form: Review your information and explain your interest.
  • One confirmed internship: Apply to suitable roles and confirm only one accepted offer.
  • Company decisions: Companies review applications and explain rejections.

03 Progress Tracking

Practical tasks. Feedback. A completed internship.

Your company supervisor assigns tasks and reviews submissions. Your academic supervisor monitors progress, and the coordinator approves completion.

  • Assigned tasks: The host assigns tasks, deadlines, criteria, and feedback.
  • Company Evaluation: The host submits the college’s required end-of-term rubric.
  • Academic monitoring: Follow progress and verified training hours.
